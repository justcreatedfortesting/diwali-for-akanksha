Dear Akanksha,

This is our first Diwali together, and our home already feels brighter than any street full of lights. This year there is one more little diya in it. Her name is Yaanvi, and she has your smile.

Thank you for the warmth you bring to every room, for your patience on the long days, and for the way you love our daughter. Watching you with her is my favourite thing in the world.

May this year bring us good health, sweet mithai, quiet mornings and loud laughter. Every diya I light tonight is for you two.

Happy Diwali, my love.

Yours, always
Pankaj and a very small Yaanvi
